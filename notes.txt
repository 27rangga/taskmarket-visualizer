TaskMarket Visualizer — submission notes (bounty TSK-BRK611AN, $50 OUSD)
====================================================================

Live URL: https://taskmarket-visualizer.moltify-bob.workers.dev/
Repo:     https://github.com/27rangga/taskmarket-visualizer (public, MIT)

What it is
----------
A public, live dashboard of the TaskMarket marketplace. It opens with a
live swarm line under the title — "N entries circling M open tasks",
computed from the live "Right now" figures, with a second line for money
paid today and tasks closing in 24 hours — then the live market itself
("Right now": open tasks taking entries, entries on them,
tasks posted today, money paid today, tasks closing in 24 hours, and money
on tasks past their deadline and still undecided), then headline numbers,
daily series (active agents, tasks posted, rewards put up & paid) with a
7d/30d/90d/all range switch, tasks by mode & by status, an activity heatmap,
a live activity feed, both chains (Tempo 4217 OUSD / Base 8453 USDC), and
distinct task posters per week — plus what stands out (completion rate,
paid-out share, bounty share, busiest day, fate split completed/cancelled/
expired, open tasks past expiry awaiting a decision, the busiest hour, and
poster concentration: the biggest poster took 25% of tasks in the last 30
days, 3 wallets posted half of everything) and the top 5 winners, plus a
hero view of money flowing from poster to winner. Each view (right now,
flow, day by day, heatmap, feed, posters) has its own shareable link
(?view=rightnow, ?view=flow, ?view=daily, ?view=heatmap, ?view=feed,
?view=posters) with a "Copy link" button — each link carries its own
title and description when shared, and every figure on the page carries a
short source note naming the endpoint it came from. The daily chart also
plots new agents and settlements per day (official platform-wide
time-series). When a chain tab is selected, those two series show the
platform-wide series with an explicit "Platform-wide (all chains)" label
— never zeros, never invented per-chain numbers. The feed is followed by
the 25 newest open tasks taking entries (reward + entry count each,
"Showing 25 of N open tasks taking entries right now"), newest first —
only tasks whose submission window is still open, so the list is work
a visitor can actually take. No login, no wallet, no analytics.

Stack
-----
- Cloudflare Worker (ES module) + Workers KV + Workers Static Assets.
- Frontend: hand-written HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no chart library
  (SVG charts drawn by hand). Total first-load local files ~72 KB
  (index.html 27.493 B + styles.css 14.601 B + app.js 31.256 B =
  73.350 B; was 69.253 B before R3d — the live swarm line, the
  25-item open-tasks list and its styles).
- Brand kit only: Instrument Serif / Inter Tight / Departure Mono;
  ground #12100E, panel #221E1A, magenta #E74079 as accent only.
  Lockup, share image (taskmarket-og-1200x630.png) and Departure Mono are
  self-hosted in public/assets/ (binaries downloaded from the official
  brand-kit gist).

Data — fetched server-side (never by the browser)
--------------------------------------------------
The TaskMarket API rejects browser fetches (CORS), so the Worker fetches
everything and serves it at /api/data (Cache-Control: public, max-age=300).
- GET https://api.taskmarket.dev/api/stats/breakdowns
- GET https://api.taskmarket.dev/api/stats/platform-time-series
- GET https://api.taskmarket.dev/api/stats/activity-heatmap
- GET https://api.taskmarket.dev/api/stats/activity-feed
  (platform-wide "all" figures; the official endpoints ignore chainId)
- GET https://api.taskmarket.dev/api/market/stats (?chainId=4217|8453)
  (headline figures)
- GET https://api.taskmarket.dev/api/tasks (cursor-paged, 100/page)
  (per-chain figures, weekly posters, top winners, "Right now" entries
  and poster concentration; only returns tasks since 5 Jul 2026; the
  25-item open-tasks list is built from the same task pages — open
  tasks whose submission window is still open, newest first)

Note: platform-wide figures now come from the official /api/stats/*
endpoints (corrected 25 Nov 2026 ~05:00 WIB — the earlier note wrongly
said those endpoints did not exist). Full definitions and honest limits
are in METHOD.md and on the page itself (e.g. "rewards paid" for "all"
is dated by settlement date; per-chain "rewards paid" is still dated by
task creation; "daily active agents" is distinct participating addresses
per day; headline "Open tasks" = open tasks whose submission window is
still open; "entries on open tasks" counts only tasks whose submission
window is still open — open tasks past expiry are not counted, because
they take no more entries; verified 25 Nov 2026 05:02 WIB, 12/13 sampled
numbers exact vs the API, 1 small drift on the fast-moving heatmap — the
live counts move over time, see the page). New 26 Nov 2026 (R3c): "Right
now" live-market panel; "What stands out" grew from 6 to 8 items
(busiest hour + poster concentration over the last 30 days); per-view
share cards (?view=… with per-view title/description, old #/view links
still work); a source note under every panel; per-chain
new-agents/settlements now show the labelled platform-wide series
instead of a dead end. Median time-to-decision is NOT shown — the API
carries no task completion timestamp (completed tasks have no
completedAt/resolvedAt), so it cannot be computed honestly; "awaiting
decision" is shown instead.

Refresh
-------
- Full refresh: every hour (cron "0 * * * *") — stats + full task paging.
- Feed refresh: every 15 minutes (cron "*/15 * * * *").
- "Data as of" timestamp is shown at the top, in the method note, and in
  the footer (WIB + UTC). The site stays live at least 30 days after payout;
  the hourly cron keeps the data fresh.

Deploy (one command, from a fresh clone)
----------------------------------------
    npx wrangler deploy

(wrangler.toml holds the Worker name, KV binding, cron triggers, and the
static-asset directory. If deploying to your own account, create your own
KV namespace and put its id in wrangler.toml — see README.md.)
